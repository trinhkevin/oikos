## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cup white, spelt, or oat flour
- 1/2 cup protein powder (60g)
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 3/4 tsp baking powder
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 1/2 cup mashed overripe banana
- 1/2 cup yogurt or additional banana
- 1/2 cup pure maple syrup, honey, or agave
- 1/3 cup oil or water
- 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
- Optional: chocolate chips, walnuts, coconut, etc.

## Instructions
1. Grease a 9x5 loaf pan and line the bottom with parchment paper. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Stir all ingredients together in a bowl.
3. Smooth the batter into the prepared pan and bake for 40 minutes.
4. Turn off the oven without opening the door, and leave the bread inside for an additional 5 minutes.
5. If a toothpick inserted in the center doesn't come out mostly clean, cover the top with foil and continue baking 5-10 minutes as needed.
6. Cool completely, then run a knife around the sides and invert onto a serving plate.
7. Cover loosely with a cloth or paper towels and store on the counter or in the refrigerator overnight for improved texture and flavor.
