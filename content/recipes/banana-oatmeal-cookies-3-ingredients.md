## Ingredients
- 1 cup rolled oats or quick oats
- 2 ripe bananas
- 1/4 cup chocolate chips
- Optional: 4 tbsp almond or peanut butter
- Optional: 1/8 tsp salt

## Instructions
1. If baking, preheat oven to 350°F. Mash the bananas in a bowl.
2. Stir in the oats, chocolate chips, and optional salt to form a dough.
3. Refrigerate until firm enough to scoop with a spoon or cookie scoop.
4. Either freeze the cookies until hard for a no-bake version, or place on a parchment-lined baking tray and bake for 20 minutes.
