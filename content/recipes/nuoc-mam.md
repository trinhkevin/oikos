## Ingredients
- 3 cups water
- 1 cup fish sauce
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup vinegar
- Garlic, chopped
- Chili, to taste

## Instructions
1. Boil the water, fish sauce, sugar, and vinegar together.
2. Keep warm and add the chopped garlic with chili.
