## Ingredients
- 8 oz (225 g) bucatini
- 12 oz (340 g) shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Juice of 1 lemon (about 2-3 tbsp)
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1/3-1/2 cup reserved pasta water

## Instructions
1. Cook the pasta: bring well-salted water to a boil, cook the bucatini until just al dente, and reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. Cook the shrimp: pat the shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat, add the shrimp in a single layer, and cook about 1-2 minutes per side until just pink. Remove to a plate.
3. Build the sauce: lower the heat to medium, add the butter, then the garlic and a pinch of red pepper flakes if using. Cook 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the lemon zest and lemon juice, then 1/4 cup of the reserved pasta water and stir.
4. Finish: add the pasta to the skillet and toss vigorously, adding more pasta water a splash at a time until glossy. Return the shrimp and any juices, and stir in the parsley. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, and lemon.

## Notes
- If you have Parmesan, add a small handful at the end — not traditional with seafood, but it tastes great.
- If you have white wine, add 1/4 cup after cooking the garlic and reduce by half before adding the lemon juice and continuing.
- For extra richness, whisk in another tablespoon of cold butter right before serving — the sauce turns silky and clings to the bucatini.
