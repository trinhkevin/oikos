## Ingredients

**Authentic Pho Broth (8+ hours)**
- 10 pounds beef bones (mix of marrow bones and bones with meat)
- 2 medium yellow onions, peeled and quartered
- 2 whole heads garlic, halved crosswise
- 4 (2-inch pieces) ginger, sliced lengthwise
- 6 whole star anise
- 12 whole cloves
- 2 whole cinnamon sticks
- 2 black cardamom pods
- 2 tablespoons fennel seeds
- 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
- 2 tablespoons salt, plus more to taste
- ⅔ cup fish sauce, divided between two pots
- 4 tablespoons sugar, divided between two pots

**Non-Authentic Pho Broth (20-30 minutes, alternative shortcut method)**
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 3 shallots, diced
- 1 bunch green onions, chopped (green and white parts divided)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced
- 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth, beef broth, or water
- 2 whole star anise
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce

**Assembly**
- 1 pound flat rice noodles, dried or fresh
- 1 pound beef (flank steak, chuck roast, brisket, or sirloin), sliced thin against the grain
- Sliced red or white onions
- Fresh cilantro, chopped

**For the Table (Garnishes)**
- Fresh mint, Thai basil
- Mung bean sprouts
- Green onions (scallions)
- Sliced jalapeño
- Lime wedges
- Thinly sliced red chilis
- Hoisin sauce
- Fish sauce
- Chili sauce
- Bok choy

## Instructions

**Authentic Pho Broth (8+ hours)**
1. Blanch the bones: Divide bones between two large stockpots and cover with cold water. Bring to a boil over high heat and simmer 15-20 minutes. Drain and rinse the bones with water.
2. Roast the bones and vegetables (optional): Preheat oven to 450°F. Transfer the bones, onion, garlic, and ginger to roasting pans without piling them. Roast for 30 minutes, toss gently, then roast an additional 15-30 minutes (45-60 minutes total).
3. Toast the spices: Add the star anise, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom pods, coriander seeds, and fennel seeds to a large dry skillet over low heat. Toast, stirring often, until fragrant, about 5 minutes. Divide the spices in half. Cut cheesecloth into two 5-inch squares, transfer the divided spices to the center of each square, gather at the top, and secure with kitchen twine.
4. Transfer the roasted bones and vegetables back to the stockpots: Divide the roasted bones, onion, garlic, and ginger between the stockpots. Scrape up the remaining bits and juices from the roasting pan with a metal spatula and add to the pot.
5. Bring to a boil: Fill each pot with about 12 cups water (until the bones are fully submerged), 1/3 cup fish sauce, and 2 tablespoons sugar. Bring to a boil.
6. Simmer the bones: Immediately reduce heat to low and simmer with the lid slightly ajar, skimming foam or excess fat occasionally. Simmer at least 6-12 hours, ideally 24 hours (do not leave the stove running overnight; cool and refrigerate, then continue simmering the next day). Add more water as needed to keep the bones submerged.
7. Strain the broth: Once the bones have simmered and the broth is ready, strain through a fine-mesh strainer into a clean pot, separating the clear broth from the bones and spices.
8. Season to taste: Add additional salt, soy sauce, fish sauce, sugar, or rock sugar to taste. Set the broth aside to cool if not using immediately and reserve the bones.
9. Don't forget about the meat: Pick the meat from the bones. Discard meat-free bones, herbs, and other bits used to make the broth, but save the meat for sandwiches, wraps, salads, or return it to the broth.
10. Skim the fat from the top (optional): Add a handful of ice to the broth to speed cooling and cover the pot with a lid. Refrigerate and allow the broth to cool fully (up to 6 hours or overnight). The result will be a hard fat layer on top and a gelatinous brown layer on the bottom. If desired, use a fork to scoop off the top fat layer.

**Non-Authentic Pho Broth (20-30 minutes, alternative shortcut method)**
11. Cook the mirepoix: In a medium stockpot over medium heat, add the olive oil and shallots. Cook the shallots about 5 minutes, stirring often. Add the white parts of the green onions, minced garlic, and ginger. Stir well and cook 1-2 more minutes.
12. Add pre-made broth or stock: Add the store-bought broth or stock to the pot and bring to a simmer.
13. Add some flavor: Add 1-2 whole star anise, the soy sauce, and fish sauce to the broth. Cover and simmer 15-20 minutes.
14. Remove the star anise from the broth.
15. Season to taste: Add more salt, sugar, fish sauce, etc., as desired.

**Assembly**
16. Slice your meat: Thinly slice the meat. If having trouble, place it in the freezer for 10 minutes to make slicing easier.
17. Noodles first: Divide the pre-cooked noodles between the bowls.
18. Add the raw meat directly on top of the rice noodles (raw/uncooked) — the hot broth will fully cook it.
19. Add the broth: Ladle the broth, boiling hot (not just warm), directly over the meat.
20. Garnish like crazy: Add extra cooked beef from the bones used to make the broth, and add one or multiple sauces to taste. Have fun with it!
