## Ingredients

**Pad Thai Sauce:**
- 2 oz (57g) seedless tamarind pulp
- ½ cup (120ml) boiling water
- 3 heaping Tbsp palm sugar, finely grated
- 2 Tbsp fish sauce
- 1 Tbsp Thai black soy sauce

**Pad Thai:**
- 1/2 lb (225g) boneless, skinless chicken breast, thinly sliced to about ⅓" (8mm) thick
- Kosher salt, to taste
- 1 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp Thai sweet soy sauce
- 8 oz (225g) dried pad Thai rice noodles
- ⅓ cup small dried shrimp
- 6 Tbsp (88ml) vegetable or canola oil, divided
- 2 shallots, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1.5 Tbsp finely minced zha cai, optional but highly recommended
- 3 large eggs, whisked together
- 1 cup Chinese garlic chives
- 1 cup mung bean sprouts, plus more to taste
- 4 Tbsp roasted peanuts, finely chopped
- 2 limes, cut into wedges

## Instructions
1. Combine tamarind pulp with the boiling water in a bowl. Let sit 1-2 minutes, then whisk until dissolved. Strain through a fine mesh sieve, pressing the pulp through with a spoon, to make the sauce base.
2. Whisk the palm sugar, fish sauce, and black soy sauce into the tamarind liquid until combined. Set the sauce aside.
3. Toss the chicken with salt, white pepper, and cornstarch in a large bowl. Add the sweet soy sauce and toss again. Set aside.
4. Soak the noodles in very hot water for 15-20 minutes until tender but al dente. Drain and set aside.
5. Process the dried shrimp in a food processor until powdered. Set aside.
6. Heat a wok over medium-high heat with 3 Tbsp oil until shimmering. Add the chicken in batches, searing 2-3 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Remove to a plate.
7. Add another 3 Tbsp oil to the wok. Once rippling hot, add the shrimp powder and stir vigorously until golden brown.
8. Add the shallots and stir-fry 2-3 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and stir-fry 2 minutes until fragrant.
9. Optionally add the zha cai and stir-fry 1-2 minutes.
10. Add the noodles and pad Thai sauce. Increase heat to high, shaking and stirring until the noodles are evenly coated and warmed through.
11. Reduce heat to medium-high. Push the noodles to one side of the wok and add the beaten eggs to the empty side. Cook 30 seconds, then scramble and stir the eggs through the noodles.
12. Add the garlic chives, bean sprouts, and cooked chicken. Stir-fry until the chives begin to wilt, then remove from heat.
13. Serve family-style or individually, garnished with peanuts, extra bean sprouts, and lime wedges.
