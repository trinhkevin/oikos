## Ingredients
- Beef
- Veggies
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp water

## Instructions
1. Season the beef and veggies.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan and sauté the beef with the minced garlic.
3. Toss in the veggies.
4. Add the sauce (1 tbsp oil, sugar, cornstarch, soy sauce, water). When the sauce has thickened, the dish is done.
