## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup rolled oats (45g)
- 1/2 cup cottage cheese (120g)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup vanilla protein powder (54g)

## Instructions
1. Add the eggs, oats, cottage cheese, and protein powder to a high-speed blender. Blend thoroughly until smooth with no clumps remaining.
2. Heat a waffle iron according to the manufacturer's directions. Once hot, pour spoonfuls of batter to cover the iron. Cook until crispy on the edges, then remove. Repeat with the remaining batter.
3. Serve immediately with your favorite sweet or savory toppings.
