## Ingredients
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1-2 tsp vinegar (to taste)
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp oil

## Instructions
1. Sauté the minced garlic in oil over medium-high heat.
2. Add the milk, peanut butter, hoisin sauce, and vinegar, and simmer until thickened.
