## Ingredients
- 5 hojicha tea bags
- 5 cups (1.2 L) hot water
- 1¼ cups (300 mL) prepared dashi (or the equivalent made with dashi concentrate)
- 5 tsp (1 tbsp + 2 tsp) soy sauce
- 3–5 tsp mirin (depending on how sweet you like it)
- Whites from 4–5 green onions, thinly sliced
- Salt, to taste (if needed)

For each bowl:
- 1 serving of hot rice (about 1 cup cooked)
- Flaked cooked salmon
- Furikake
- Nori strips
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions
1. Heat the water until it's about 185–195°F (85–90°C) — just below boiling.
2. Steep the hojicha tea bags for 2–3 minutes, then remove them.
3. Stir in the dashi, soy sauce, and mirin.
4. Add the sliced green onion whites and let them infuse for 1 minute.
5. Taste. The broth should be light, savory, and slightly roasted from the hojicha. Adjust with a little more soy sauce or dashi if you'd like a stronger flavor.
6. For each bowl, top the hot rice with flaked salmon, furikake, nori strips, and sesame seeds, then ladle 1 to 1¼ cups of the hot broth over the top.
7. Since this makes a larger batch, keep the broth warm over very low heat after steeping the tea — that way everyone can assemble their own bowl and the rice won't get mushy while waiting.
