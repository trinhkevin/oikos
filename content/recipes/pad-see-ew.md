## Ingredients
- 200g / 7 oz dried wide rice stick noodles, or 15 oz / 450g fresh wide flat rice noodles (Sen Yai)
- 2 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tsp white vinegar
- 2 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp peanut or vegetable oil (separated)
- 2 cloves garlic, very finely chopped
- 1 cup / 150g / 5 oz chicken thighs (boneless, skinless), sliced
- 1 large egg
- 4 stems Chinese broccoli

## Instructions
1. Trim the ends off the Chinese broccoli and cut into 7.5cm/3" pieces. Separate the leaves from the stems, and cut any thick stems in half vertically.
2. Cook the noodles according to the packet directions and drain, timing it so they're ready just before you need them.
3. Mix the sauce ingredients together until the sugar dissolves.
4. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large heavy-based skillet or wok over high heat.
5. Add the garlic and cook for 15 seconds, then add the chicken and cook until it mostly changes from pink to white.
6. Add the Chinese broccoli stems and cook until the chicken is nearly cooked through.
7. Add the Chinese broccoli leaves and cook until just wilted.
8. Push everything to one side of the wok, crack the egg in, and scramble it.
9. Remove the chicken and vegetables onto a plate, then scrape the wok clean.
10. Return the wok to the stove and heat the remaining 2 tbsp oil over high heat until just starting to smoke. Add the noodles and sauce, and toss for 1 to 1½ minutes until the sauce is dispersed and the noodle edges start to caramelize.
11. Quickly add the chicken and vegetables back in and toss to disperse. Serve immediately.
