## Ingredients
- 2 1/2 lbs pork belly
- 1/3 cup fish sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons chicken or mushroom bouillon powder (optional)
- 1 teaspoon MSG (optional)
- 4 garlic cloves, finely diced
- 1 large shallot, finely diced
- 1/2 teaspoon thick soy sauce or homemade caramel cooking sauce
- 3 cups water
- 12 oz coconut soda (1 can)
- 9–12 chicken eggs
- Red chilies or thinly sliced green onions, to garnish (optional)

## Instructions
1. Cut the pork belly into 1½ x 1 inch pieces. Place in a large pot with enough water to cover and bring to a boil, about 9 minutes. Skim off impurities from the surface, then transfer the pork to a colander and rinse until clean.
2. Transfer the cleaned pork to a bowl and marinate with the fish sauce, salt, sugar, black pepper, bouillon powder, MSG, garlic, shallot, and thick soy sauce for at least 30 minutes, or overnight.
3. Bring the reserved blanching water back to a boil. Carefully lower in the eggs and cook for 10 minutes. Transfer to a bowl, gently crack the shells, add ice water, and peel while still warm. Set aside.
4. Wash the pot and return it to the stove. Add the marinated pork along with its juices and pan-fry for 1 minute to release the aromatics. Add the water and coconut soda. Simmer uncovered over medium heat for 40 minutes.
5. Add the boiled eggs to the pot and continue simmering uncovered for 30 minutes, pushing the eggs down into the liquid periodically.
6. Garnish with red chilies or green onions and additional black pepper if desired. Serve with steamed rice.
