## Ingredients
- 3 lb beef shank
- 3 large Yukon Gold potatoes
- 2 large carrots
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1/2 onion
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 15 oz tomato sauce
- 2 quarts water
- 1 tbsp beef bouillon
- 2 dried bay leaves
- 1/2 inch nub rock sugar
- 1/2 tbsp MSG
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 tbsp cornstarch (optional, for slurry)
- 2 tbsp water (optional, for slurry)
- Cooked jasmine rice, for serving
- Vietnamese baguettes, for serving
- Cilantro, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Dice the onion and mince the garlic. Peel the carrots and potatoes and cut into 1-inch pieces. Cut the beef shank into 1.5-inch pieces.
2. Heat oil in a heavy pot over high heat. Sear the beef on all sides until browned, then remove and set aside.
3. Add the garlic and onion to the pot; sauté until softened and fragrant. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1-2 minutes.
4. Return the beef to the pot. Add the tomato sauce, water, beef bouillon, and bay leaves. Season with rock sugar, MSG, and salt.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 2 hours, until the beef is tender.
6. Add the potatoes and carrots; continue simmering 10-15 minutes, until fork-tender.
7. Stir in the frozen peas. Season to taste with fish sauce and black pepper. Optionally stir in the cornstarch slurry to thicken.
8. Serve over jasmine rice or with Vietnamese baguettes. Garnish with cilantro.
