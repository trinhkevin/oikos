## Ingredients
- 8 oz spaghetti or linguine
- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup reserved pasta water
- Parmesan and chopped parsley, to finish

## Instructions
1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water until just shy of al dente,
   reserving a cup of the starchy water before draining.
2. Melt the butter in a wide skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic
   and red pepper flakes, and cook gently until the garlic is fragrant
   and just turning golden — don't let it brown.
3. Add the pasta and a splash of the reserved water to the skillet.
   Toss over medium heat, adding more water as needed, until the sauce
   clings to the noodles.
4. Serve immediately with Parmesan and parsley.
