## Ingredients
- 8 ounces buckwheat noodles (memil guksu) or wheat flour noodles (somyeon/somen)
- 1 small cucumber (about 3 ounces)
- 1 small carrot (about 3 ounces)
- 2 ounces red cabbage leaves
- 2 ounces lettuce leaves
- 1/2 cup thinly sliced kimchi
- 1 boiled egg (optional)
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 teaspoons sesame seeds
- 3 tablespoons gochujang (Korean chili pepper paste)
- 1 tablespoon gochugaru (Korean chili pepper flakes)
- 1 teaspoon gyeoja (Korean/Asian hot mustard paste, optional)
- 2 tablespoons sugar (or corn syrup/oligo syrup)
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons soft drink (ginger ale, Sprite, etc.), apple juice, or orange juice (or 1 tablespoon Korean plum syrup/maesilcheong, optional)

## Instructions
1. Mix all sauce ingredients together and refrigerate; the sauce keeps up to 2 weeks.
2. Julienne the cucumber, carrot, and red cabbage, and thinly slice the lettuce. Keep the vegetables chilled.
3. Boil water in a medium pot and cook the noodles according to the package directions.
4. Drain and rinse the noodles in icy cold water until very cold. Form into two mounds and place in a colander to drain.
5. Place the noodles in the center of a serving bowl. Arrange the cold vegetables over the noodles. Drizzle with sesame oil, sprinkle with sesame seeds, and top with half a boiled egg. Serve the sauce on top or on the side.
6. Alternatively, toss the noodles with the sauce, sesame oil, and vegetables until well combined. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and top with half a boiled egg.
