## Ingredients
- ½–¾ lb boneless skinless chicken breast (about 1–2 breasts)
- 1 cup white rice, rinsed
- ½ tablespoon cooking oil
- ½ onion, sliced
- 2–3 cloves garlic, minced (about ½ tbsp)
- 1½" knob ginger, peeled and grated (about 1 tbsp)
- 3 cups unsalted or low sodium chicken broth, plus more to finish
- 3 cups water
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- Kosher salt and pepper, to taste
- Optional toppings: cilantro, thinly sliced green onion, fried shallots, chili oil, bean sprouts, soft-boiled eggs

## Instructions
1. Season the chicken breast with 1½ teaspoons salt and ½ teaspoon pepper all over. Set aside.
2. Turn on Sauté (Normal) mode on the Instant Pot. Once it reads "Hot," add the oil and let it heat for a couple of seconds.
3. Add the onion and a pinch of salt; cook 1–2 minutes to soften lightly, then stir in the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds until fragrant. Turn off the Instant Pot. Add a splash of water to deglaze if needed, scraping up any browned bits.
4. Add the rice, chicken broth, water, fish sauce, 1 teaspoon salt, and ½ teaspoon pepper; stir to combine. Nestle the chicken breasts in the middle, ensuring they are fully submerged.
5. Seal the Instant Pot. Set to Pressure Cook (Normal) mode for 10 minutes. Let pressure release naturally for 5 minutes, then quick release the rest.
6. Carefully remove the lid. Transfer the chicken breasts to a cutting board and shred, then return the shredded chicken to the Instant Pot.
7. Stir vigorously, adding more chicken broth if you prefer a looser consistency. Season to taste with additional fish sauce and salt/pepper.
8. Serve in bowls topped with freshly cracked pepper and desired toppings.
