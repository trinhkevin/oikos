## Ingredients
- 2 lb large head of gai choi (Asian mustard greens)
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 16 cups water
- 4 tbsp salt
- 10 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp vinegar

## Instructions
1. Break apart the gai choi leaves and clean thoroughly under running water. Dry on the counter for 10-16 hours, or in the sun for 6-8 hours.
2. Cut the leaves and onions into desired size, about 1-inch pieces.
3. Combine water, salt, sugar, and vinegar in a pot and bring almost to a boil. Turn off heat and let cool until the water is warm but touchable without burning yourself.
4. Add all the vegetables to the pot, ensuring everything is submerged (or transfer to a jar instead). Place the container in a warm location, such as a window sill or an oven with just the oven light on, to pickle. Taste a piece every 24 hours until it reaches your desired sourness, then transfer to the refrigerator.
