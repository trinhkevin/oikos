## Ingredients
- 1 16 oz can of chickpeas
- 3-4 tablespoons lemon juice (depending on taste)
- 1 1/2 tablespoons tahini
- 2-3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup water

## Instructions
1. Soak the chickpeas to loosen the skins and wash away the salt.
2. Put all ingredients in a food processor for 15 minutes. You need that much time to break the remaining skins down.
3. Check if it's ready: after 15 minutes, turn the food processor container sideways — it should move slowly. Spoon into a bowl. Garnish with olive oil and paprika. Serve with pita cut into triangles or fresh veggie spears. Sahtain!
