## Ingredients

**Bisque**
- 4 lobster tails
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, peeled and finely chopped
- 2 stalks celery, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme, chopped
- 1 teaspoon fresh tarragon, chopped (plus more for serving)
- 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cracked pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 1/4 cups dry white wine or sherry
- 4 cups lobster stock (or seafood/fish stock)
- 3/4 – 1 cup heavy cream

**Garlic Butter Lobster Meat**
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Pinch of salt
- Pinch of pepper
- Pinch of cayenne pepper

**For the Stock**
- 5 cups water
- 1 teaspoon sea salt

## Instructions
1. Fill a large pot with 5 cups water, stir in 1 teaspoon sea salt, and bring to a boil.
2. Add the lobster tails, cover, and boil for 5 minutes until bright red.
3. Remove the tails, reserving the cooking liquid. When cool enough to handle, remove the meat from the shells, keeping any juices; return the shells to the pot.
4. Bring the water back to a boil with the shells, then reduce to medium-low and simmer for 15 minutes to extract flavor for the stock.
5. While the stock simmers, chop the lobster meat into bite-sized pieces and refrigerate until needed.
6. In a large pot, heat the butter and olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, celery, thyme, and tarragon; cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Season with the bouillon powder, salt, cracked pepper, and cayenne. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
7. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute to coat the vegetables. Sprinkle in the flour and cook for 2 minutes, stirring occasionally.
8. Pour in the wine and simmer until reduced by half. Add the 4 cups lobster stock and simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened and the flavors have melded.
9. Remove from the heat and blend until smooth using a blender or immersion blender. Return to medium-low heat and stir in the heavy cream.
10. In a separate skillet, melt the butter for the garlic butter lobster meat over medium heat and sauté the garlic for 30 seconds until fragrant.
11. Add the chopped lobster meat, season with a pinch each of salt, pepper, and cayenne, and sauté lightly for 1 minute, stirring, until warmed through.
12. Stir three-quarters of the garlic butter lobster meat into the bisque. Ladle into bowls, top each with the remaining lobster meat and fresh tarragon, and serve.
